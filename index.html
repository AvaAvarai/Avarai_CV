About Me

I solve hard problems with clean math, clear visuals, and code that doesn’t lie.

I am a recent bachelor of science graduate with a double major in Computer Science and Applied Mathematics, working as an AI/ML Research Assistant, AI Ed-Tech Startup Founder, and AI/ML Consultant.

My work focuses on visualization-driven machine learning, merging computation with human intuition to create transparent, interactive, and trustworthy artificial intelligence systems. I have developed methods in synthetic data generation, boosting classification models, and multidimensional clustering, alongside advancing feature engineering, visual knowledge discovery, and classification refinement. My approach bridges the gap between cutting-edge research and real-world impact, ensuring AI models are both interpretable and high-performing.

My work experience spans system administration, full-stack software development, and academic AI/ML research. I approach every challenge with a mindset of deconstructing complex problems, building modular tools, and verifying every result reproducibly.
